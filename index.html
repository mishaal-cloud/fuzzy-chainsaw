📚 What is SSR Survey Simulation?

This tool implements the Semantic Similarity Rating (SSR) methodology from research paper arXiv:2510.08338. Instead of analyzing single responses, it simulates realistic survey panels by aggregating 50-100+ synthetic consumer responses. Achieves 90% of human test-retest reliability when demographics are varied.

Use Case: Screen product concepts, test customer satisfaction, or evaluate survey questions BEFORE conducting expensive human surveys. Works with any AI tool and any survey topic.
