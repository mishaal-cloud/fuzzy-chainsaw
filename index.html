📚 What is SSR?

Semantic Similarity Rating (SSR) is a research-backed method from arXiv:2510.08338 that converts natural language responses into Likert scale ratings (1-5). Instead of asking AI models for direct numerical ratings, SSR elicits textual responses and maps them using semantic similarity to reference anchor statements, producing more realistic and reliable rating distributions.
